Welcome to Tinungva

A Sanctuary in the Misty Valleys

Perched on the gorgeous ridges of Bandarban along the scenic Chimbuk Road, Tinungva Resort & Restaurant is your perfect gateway to experience the wilderness. Founded and managed by Stephen Bawm, our resort is built to coexist with nature, offering traditional wooden architecture and custom services that let you unwind completely.

Here, you wake up above the clouds, breathe the fresh mountain air, and savor recipes handed down through generations, combining the finest Bengali traditions with indigenous hill cuisine.
